## 👋 About Me

I am an undergraduate student majoring in **Mechanical Design, Manufacturing and Automation** at **Ocean University of China (OUC)**, and an incoming M.S. student at the **Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA)**. I also conduct research at **RoboParty Lab**.

My research focuses on **embodied intelligence and robot learning**, especially how robots acquire, combine, and execute skills in dynamic environments. I am interested in connecting **reinforcement learning, whole-body control, and high-level decision-making** to build more capable physical agents.

**Research interests**

- **Humanoid skill learning:** whole-body control, motion tracking, and sim-to-real transfer.
- **Skill composition:** reusable motion representations and long-horizon task execution.
- **Interactive robotics:** robot table tennis, motor learning, and strategic self-play.
- **Bio-inspired robotics:** learning-based locomotion and autonomous navigation.

## 🔬 Selected Publications & Research

### 🤸 CHOREO: Every Humanoid Skill as a Trajectory

**arXiv preprint, 2026 · Co-first author**

A training-free framework that unifies heterogeneous humanoid skills as executable trajectories and composes them into longer behaviors. Evaluated on **130 multi-action tasks** with the Unitree G1 in MuJoCo.

[**Paper**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22274) · [**Project Page**](https://ziyisun85-ops.github.io/Project-choreo/)

### 🏓 From Hitter to Player: Separating Motor Execution and Strategy in Competitive Humanoid Table Tennis

**arXiv preprint, 2026 · Co-first author**

A hierarchical approach that separates target-conditioned whole-body striking from learned strategic target selection. Demonstrated **multi-stroke rallies and full games against a human opponent** on a real humanoid robot.

[**Paper**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14067) · [**Project Page**](https://mufanmufan.github.io/hitter-to-player/)

### 🐟 A Two-Stage Learning Framework for Autonomous Obstacle Avoidance of an Eel-Like Robot

**IEEE RCAR 2026 · First author · Best Paper Finalist**

A two-stage framework combining imitation learning and reinforcement learning for autonomous obstacle avoidance, incorporating bio-inspired motion control and sim-to-real evaluation.

[**Paper (PDF)**](https://ziyisun85-ops.github.io/assets/files/A_Two_Stage_Learning_Framework_for_Autonomous_Obstacle_Avoidance_of_an_Eel_Like_Robot.pdf)
