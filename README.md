## 👋 About Me

I am an undergraduate student majoring in **Mechanical Design, Manufacturing and Automation** at **Ocean University of China (OUC)**, and an incoming M.S. student at the **Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA)**. I also conduct research at **RoboParty Lab**.

My research focuses on **agentic robotics, humanoid perception and interaction, and tactile manipulation**. I am interested in enabling robots to plan and execute long-horizon tasks, adapt their actions through interaction, and use visual and tactile feedback to operate reliably in the real world.

**Research interests**

- **Agentic Robotics:** task planning, skill composition, execution monitoring, and failure recovery for autonomous long-horizon tasks.
- **Humanoid Perception & Interaction:** perception-driven whole-body control and adaptive interaction with people, objects, and dynamic environments.
- **Tactile Manipulation:** visuo-tactile learning and tactile-guided dexterous manipulation, with an emphasis on contact-rich tasks and dynamic object handling.
