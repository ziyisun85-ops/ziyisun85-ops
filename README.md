## 👋 About Me

I am an undergraduate student majoring in **Mechanical Design, Manufacturing and Automation** at **Ocean University of China (OUC)**, and an incoming M.S. student at the **Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA)**. I also conduct research at **RoboParty Lab**.

My research focuses on **embodied intelligence and robot learning**, with particular interests in **agentic robotics, humanoid perception and interaction, and tactile manipulation**.

I am interested in developing intelligent robotic systems that can **understand their environments, autonomously plan and execute complex tasks, and adapt through physical interaction**. My goal is to bridge high-level intelligence with low-level robotic control, enabling robots to operate reliably in dynamic and unstructured real-world environments.

### 🔭 Research Interests

- **Agentic Robotics:** Autonomous task planning, skill composition, long-horizon execution, and failure recovery for embodied agents.

- **Humanoid Perception & Interaction:** Perception-driven whole-body control, reinforcement learning, and adaptive interaction with humans and dynamic environments.

- **Tactile Manipulation:** Visuo-tactile learning, contact-rich manipulation, and tactile-guided control for dexterous and dynamic tasks.

## 🔬 Selected Publications & Research

### 🤸 CHOREO: Every Humanoid Skill as a Trajectory

**Ziyi Sun**<sup>*</sup>, Jingwen Chen<sup>*</sup>, Yuxi Wang, Xiuze Xia, Long Cheng, Zhaoxiang Zhang, Yujun Dong

A training-free framework that unifies heterogeneous humanoid skills as executable trajectories and composes them into longer behaviors.

Evaluated on **130 multi-action tasks** with the Unitree G1 in MuJoCo, demonstrating scalable composition of pretrained humanoid capabilities.

[**Paper**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22274) · [**Project Page**](https://ziyisun85-ops.github.io/Project-choreo/)

### 🏓 From Hitter to Player: Separating Motor Execution and Strategy in Competitive Humanoid Table Tennis

Fanrong Dong<sup>*</sup>, **Ziyi Sun**<sup>*</sup>, Ziqi Han, Junhan Sun, Lei Ye, Zetong Jing, Hao Zhao, Shaoxun Liu, Rongrong Wang

A hierarchical framework that separates target-conditioned whole-body motor execution from learned strategic decision-making in competitive humanoid table tennis.

Through motor self-play and strategic learning, the system enables adaptive ball placement and demonstrates **multi-stroke rallies and complete games against a human opponent** on a real humanoid robot.

[**Paper**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14067) · [**Project Page**](https://mufanmufan.github.io/hitter-to-player/)

### 🐟 A Two-Stage Learning Framework for Autonomous Obstacle Avoidance of an Eel-Like Robot

**Ziyi Sun**, Luwen Li, Changhong He, Shuo Jiang, Zhuoyan Wang, Haoyuan Cheng

**IEEE RCAR 2026 · Best Paper Finalist**

A two-stage learning framework combining imitation learning and reinforcement learning for autonomous obstacle avoidance in bio-inspired eel-like robots.

The approach integrates learning-based navigation with bio-inspired locomotion control and investigates sim-to-real transfer for autonomous robotic systems.

[**Paper (PDF)**](https://ziyisun85-ops.github.io/assets/files/A_Two_Stage_Learning_Framework_for_Autonomous_Obstacle_Avoidance_of_an_Eel_Like_Robot.pdf)
