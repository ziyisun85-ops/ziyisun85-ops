### 🤖 About Me

I am an undergraduate student majoring in **Mechanical Design, Manufacturing and Automation** at **Ocean University of China (OUC)**, and have been recommended for admission to pursue an M.S. degree at the **Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA)**. I am also a member of **RoboParty LAB**.

My research focuses on **embodied intelligence** — perception, decision-making, learning-based control, and long-horizon task execution for physical agents, with a particular interest in humanoid and biomimetic robots operating in complex real-world environments.

- 🔭 Currently exploring: humanoid skill learning, robot table tennis, and modular motor control
- 🎓 Incoming: M.Sc. at CASIA (2026)
- 💼 Research intern at RoboParty LAB
- 📫 How to reach me: **ziyisun85@gmail.com**
